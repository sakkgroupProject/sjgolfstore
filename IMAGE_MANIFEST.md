## 5. Replacing images with your own photos

- **Per product (no code):** /admin → Products → [product] → Media → Image URLs — first line is
  the main photo, the rest are gallery shots. Save and the storefront updates instantly.
- **Homepage / categories:** /admin → Content (hero, story) or edit the `IMG` map in
  `src/db/seed.ts` for category cards, then `npx tsx scripts/setup.ts --reset`.
- **Self-host:** put files in `public/images/`, point any URL at `/images/your-photo.jpg`
  (admin or seed).

Recommended sizes: product cards 4:5 (1200×1500), heroes 16:9 (1920×1080), neutral
backgrounds, consistent lighting.
